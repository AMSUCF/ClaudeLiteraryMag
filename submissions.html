Submission Guidelines

What We're Looking For

Claude Literary Magazine seeks exceptional literary writing that demonstrates craft, originality, and voice. We publish:

  • Fiction: Short stories up to 7,000 words
  • Poetry: Up to 5 poems per submission
  • Creative Nonfiction: Essays and memoirs up to 5,000 words

Submission Requirements

  • Previously unpublished work only
  • Simultaneous submissions accepted (please notify us immediately if your work is accepted elsewhere)
  • One submission per genre per reading period
  • Standard manuscript format: double-spaced, 12-point font, 1-inch margins
  • Include a brief third-person bio (100 words or less)
  • Response time: 8-12 weeks

Reading Periods

  • General Submissions: January 1 - March 31, August 1 - October 31
  • Themed Issues: Check our website for special calls

AI Policy

We accept only human-authored work. Please see our complete AI policy for details on ethical use of AI tools during the writing process.

Rights

We acquire First North American Serial Rights. All other rights revert to the author upon publication. We request that you acknowledge first publication in Claude Literary Magazine in future reprints.

What Happens Next?

  1. You'll receive an automated confirmation email
  2. Your submission enters our blind review process
  3. Multiple editors review your work

  5. If accepted, we'll work with you on any final edits before publication
